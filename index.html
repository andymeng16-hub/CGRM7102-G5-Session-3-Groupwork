Before you go · 先看潮汐

The sandbar has
a schedule.

The tombolo that links Sharp Island to Kiu Tau is submerged at high tide — generally when the tidal height is above 1.25 m, according to the board we photographed. On our survey date, 13 November 2026, the printed emergence window was only 08:00–10:00.

Our first field records start at 11:10, after that window had closed. Photo p02 shows the shingle bar only partly above water. That single piece of evidence is why the two routes below are so different: one has to be timed, the other does not.

How much sun
are you walking in?

At every point we looked at about 20 m of the existing path and used the same three categories throughout. Reading left to right gives you the exposure sequence of each walk.

Weather on the day: overcast at 11:10, sunny with scattered cumulus from about 12:47. Shade was recorded, not inferred from the weather.
