BEFORE YOU GO先看潮汐

THE SANDBAR HAS
A SCHEDULE.连岛沙洲有时间表

The tombolo that links Sharp Island to Kiu Tau is submerged at high tide, generally when the tidal height is above 1.25 m, according to the board we photographed. On our survey date, 13 NOVEMBER 2026, the printed emergence window was only 08:00 to 10:00.

潮高超过约 1.25 米时沙洲没入水中；调查当日的露出时段只有上午 8 时至 10 时，我们 11:10 才抵达，因此只从岸边观察。

Our first field records start at 11:10, after that window had closed. Photo p02 shows the shingle bar only partly above water. We timed the coastal stage around the tide and kept the rest of the traverse on the main island.

TOMBOLO AND TIDE连岛沙洲与潮汐

A BRIDGE THAT
COMES AND GOES.一座时有时无的桥

The bar between the main island and Kiu Tau is neither land nor sea. Set a tide height and the model floods the saddle, lifts the crest clear, and redraws the waterline along the way. Contours are drawn every half metre, so you can read the shape of the ridge before the water reaches it.

主岛与桥头之间的沙洲既非陆地也非海面。设定潮高，模型会把鞍部淹入水中或托出水面，并沿程重绘水线；等高线每半米一条，可在水淹之前先读出沙脊的形状。

VEGETATION植被

FOUR WAYS TO
BE A PLANT HERE.四种生存方式

Sharp Island repeats four plant formations. Each one answers the same four pressures in a different way: wind, salt, thin soil and fire. The plates are drawn from published Hong Kong vegetation accounts rather than from a quadrat survey.

桥咀洲的植被可归为四类重复出现的群落，各自以不同方式回应风、盐、薄土与山火。插画依据香港植被文献绘制，并非样方调查结果。

FIRE, NOT CLIMATE, DECIDES WHETHER SHRUB OR GRASS WINS THE SLOPE.决定山坡是灌丛还是草坡的，是山火而非气候。

SPECIES LISTS FOLLOW PUBLISHED HONG KONG VEGETATION ACCOUNTS AND THE AGRICULTURE, FISHERIES AND CONSERVATION DEPARTMENT. PLATES ARE GROUP-DRAWN.物种名录依据香港植被文献与渔农自然护理署资料；插画为本组绘制。
