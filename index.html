BEFORE YOU GO出发之前

THE SANDBAR HAS
A SCHEDULE.连岛沙洲
有时间表

The tombolo that links Sharp Island to Kiu Tau is submerged at high tide, generally when the tidal height is above 1.25 m, according to the board we photographed. On our survey date, 13 NOVEMBER 2026, the printed emergence window was only 08:00 to 10:00.连接桥咀洲与桥头的连岛沙洲在高潮时没入水中，按我们拍下的告示牌，潮高超过 1.25 米 即可淹没。调查当日 2026 年 11 月 13 日，告示牌印出的露出时段只有 上午 8 时至 10 时。

Our first field records start at 11:10, after that window had closed. Photo p02 shows the shingle bar only partly above water. We timed the coastal stage around the tide and kept the rest of the traverse on the main island.我们的首笔记录始于 11:10，此时露出时段已经结束。照片 p02 显示沙石堤只有部分露出水面。我们按潮位为海岸段定了时间，其后大部分路程仍在主岛上完成。

TOMBOLO AND TIDE连岛沙洲与潮汐

A BRIDGE THAT
COMES AND GOES.一座时有时无的桥

OpenStreetMap draws this bar as one beach outline, and the outline is what the panel uses. It runs 272 m from the south shore of Sharp Island across to Kiu Tau Chau, the small island on the far side. At the Kiu Tau end it is only three metres of gravel. Through the middle it holds ten to thirteen. Where it fans into the beach it reaches thirty three. Set a tide height and the model floods the ridge, lifts it clear again, and redraws the waterline from the measured outline. Contours every half metre. The dashed red line is the 1.25 m limit on the pier board.沙洲在 OpenStreetMap 里是一条完整的砾石滩轮廓，这个面板用的就是它。轮廓从桥咀洲南岸一直伸到对岸的桥头岛，全长 272 米。靠桥头岛那一端只有 3 米宽的砾石，中段保持在 10 到 13 米，展开进沙滩的地方到 33 米。拖动潮高，模型会把沙脊淹入水中或重新托出水面，并按实测轮廓重绘水线。等高线每半米一条，红色虚线是码头告示牌上的 1.25 米上限。

THE PROFILE IS THE WALK ITSELF. Ground is CGIAR SRTM 30 m resampled onto a seven and a half metre grid. The sun is placed with the NOAA position algorithm for 13 November 2026, and each position along the path is tested at the minute the group reached it, between 11:10 and 13:30. The share is the part of the sun that gets past terrain and canopy to head height: a ridge in the way takes it to nothing at once, and canopy along the ray takes it down gradually. A position is unshaded above 0.9, partly shaded between 0.4 and 0.9, and mostly shaded below 0.4, which are the thresholds the field categories were read against. VEGETATION植被

FOUR WAYS TO
BE A PLANT HERE.四种生存方式

Sharp Island repeats four plant formations. Each one answers the same four pressures in a different way: wind, salt, thin soil and fire. The plates are drawn from published Hong Kong vegetation accounts rather than from a quadrat survey.桥咀洲的植被可归为四类重复出现的群落，各自以不同方式回应风、盐、薄土与山火。插画依据香港植被文献绘制，并非样方调查结果。

FIRE, NOT CLIMATE, DECIDES WHETHER SHRUB OR GRASS WINS THE SLOPE.决定山坡是灌丛还是草坡的，是山火而非气候。

SPECIES LISTS FOLLOW PUBLISHED HONG KONG VEGETATION ACCOUNTS AND THE AGRICULTURE, FISHERIES AND CONSERVATION DEPARTMENT. PLATES ARE GROUP-DRAWN.物种名录依据香港植被文献与渔农自然护理署资料；插画为本组绘制。
