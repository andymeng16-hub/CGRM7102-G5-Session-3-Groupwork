BEFORE YOU GO ／先看潮汐

THE SANDBAR HAS
A SCHEDULE.连岛沙洲有时间表

The tombolo that links Sharp Island to Kiu Tau is submerged at high tide — generally when the tidal height is above 1.25 m, according to the board we photographed. On our survey date, 13 NOVEMBER 2026, the printed emergence window was only 08:00–10:00.

潮高超过约 1.25 米时沙洲没入水中；调查当日的露出时段只有上午 8 时至 10 时，我们 11:10 才抵达，因此只从岸边观察。

Our first field records start at 11:10, after that window had closed. Photo p02 shows the shingle bar only partly above water. That single piece of evidence is why the coastal stage of the route has to be timed, and why the rest of the traverse stays on the main island.
